About Modern Eyes

We offer many eye care services, from routine exams to treatment of eye diseases. Dr. Sturdy and our highly skilled team welcome you to our practice, where we strive to make patients feel at home. This personal care and individualized attention is why people throughout the Denver metro area come to Modern Eyes for all their vision health needs.

Our goal is to provide dedicated and friendly eye care. We'll cater to your individual vision needs, answer any eye care questions you have, and supply you with the most up-to-date optometric experience and fashion eyewear to keep your eyes clear and healthy. We are committed to excellence in serving your family's complete eye care needs.

Each member of our team is selected by Dr. Sturdy for their experience, professional demeanor and dedication to personalized care. We’ll do our best to find appointment times that fit your busy schedule, help you understand your vision insurance coverage, and provide financial alternatives so you get the best vision care at an affordable price. In many cases, your exam is completely drop-free. Ultra-wide-field retinal imaging is our standard.

Meet our optometrists

Dr. Sturdy, Dr. Keesey and Dr. Anderson.

Benjamin Sturdy, O.D.

Diplomate, American Board of Optometry

Dr. Sturdy graduated with distinction from Southern College of Optometry in 2009 after earning his bachelor's degree in human biology from the University of Kansas. He then completed his residency in ocular disease under Dr. Paul Ajamian at OMNI Eye Services in Atlanta, GA, and completed additional requirements and testing to become a Diplomate of the American Board of Optometry.

He started practicing in Colorado in 2010 at ICON Eye Care as the Director of Education and Quality. In a commitment to advance the optometric profession, he created ICON's first accredited residency position in coordination with the Pacific University College of Optometry and served as the Residency Coordinator. In late 2021, Dr. Sturdy acquired New Visions and Opticians 2 from Dr. Clausen and Kathy Fincher. He is committed to providing comprehensive eye care using state-of-the-art technology to achieve the best possible outcomes for his patients.

Outside of his passion for vision, Dr. Sturdy enjoys exploring Colorado with his family, including four children!

  • Education: Southern College of Optometry (with distinction, 2009); University of Kansas, bachelor’s degree in human biology
  • Residency: Ocular disease, OMNI Eye Services, Atlanta
  • Credential: Diplomate, American Board of Optometry

Tara Keesey, O.D.

Optometrist

Dr. Keesey comes to Modern Eyes with over 16 years of experience providing comprehensive eye care in a private practice setting, including the treatment and management of anterior and posterior segment eye disease and glaucoma, ocular emergencies, foreign body removal, contact lenses, pediatric eye exams, and post-operative surgical co-management.

Her clinic experience also includes training at the University of Chicago's Duchossois Center for Advanced Medicine (DCAM) Eye Center, the Veterans Administration Medical Center in Battle Creek, Michigan, and Wesley-Jessen's VisionCare Specialists Contact Lens and Eye Care Center in Des Plaines, Illinois.

Dr. Keesey graduated cum laude from the University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign with a B.S. in Liberal Arts and Sciences with a concentration in bioengineering. She received her Doctorate of Optometry from the Illinois College of Optometry, graduating summa cum laude as her class valedictorian. Her honors include the Valedictory Award, the Presidential Scholarship, the Beta Sigma Kappa Medal, and induction into the Beta Sigma Kappa International Optometric Honor Society and the Tomb and Key Honor Fraternity.

In her spare time, Dr. Keesey enjoys skiing, playing tennis, and spending time with her family.

Kevin Anderson, O.D.

Optometrist · Colorado Board Certified Therapeutic Optometrist

Dr. Anderson attended the University of Nebraska-Lincoln and Western Illinois University for undergraduate studies, then transferred to the Illinois College of Optometry and graduated with a Doctor of Optometry degree in 1987. He began caring for patients of the Optical Gallery on January 4, 1988 — a long history of excellent care that continues at Modern Eyes!

Dr. Anderson is committed to providing the most thorough and comprehensive primary eye care in Colorado, using the latest technology and professional continuing education to remain on the forefront of eye care. Educating and inspiring patients with a complete understanding of their eye health is his priority number one. He practices full-scope primary eye care.

  • Subspecialties: Specialty contact lenses, pre- and post-op care for laser vision correction, dry eye syndrome and keratoconus
