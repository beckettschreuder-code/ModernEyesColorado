What we offer

Eye care for everyday life.

Quality comprehensive eye exams for adults and children, plus everything that comes after. Tap a service to learn more.

Services

01 Comprehensive eye exams

Regular eye exams are an invaluable tool for keeping eyes healthy by detecting and preventing disease. Some diseases develop slowly without causing pain or vision loss, and early detection can reduce the risk of further harm and allow for a choice of treatment options.

Your exam starts with a conversation about any symptoms, medications, work environment, overall health and family history. We then check your vision and prescription, test how your eyes focus, move and work together (including depth perception, color vision and peripheral vision), and examine the health of your eyes, including eye pressure. The whole process can take about an hour, depending on your needs.

02 Drop-free exams & retinal imaging

Modern Eyes provides a completely drop-free eye care experience in many cases, using retinal imaging, corneal topography, visual field screening, iCare tonometry (eye pressure), and other front- and back-of-the-eye health equipment.

For many patients, that means no dilating drops and a more comfortable visit.

03 Contact lens exams & fittings

Thanks to advances in lens technology, almost everyone is a candidate for contact lenses — including patients with astigmatism and those who prefer bifocal or multifocal lenses.

After your eye exam, we'll help you choose the type that fits your vision and lifestyle: daily disposables, extended-wear soft lenses, or rigid gas permeable lenses. Custom and cosmetic lenses are available too, from natural-looking color changes to lenses that camouflage an irregularity.

04 Hard-to-fit & specialty contacts

Some eye conditions make wearing contacts harder, but that doesn't rule them out. We fit specialized lenses for patients with:

  • Astigmatism — custom toric lenses that stay aligned on the eye
  • Keratoconus — gas permeable lenses that support the cornea
  • Presbyopia — bifocal, multifocal or monovision lenses
  • Dry eyes — lens choices plus medicated drops to keep you comfortable
  • Giant papillary conjunctivitis (GPC) — gas permeable lenses that resist protein buildup
05 Glasses, frames & sunglasses

For patients who need glasses or contact lenses, we offer a complete optical department with a wide selection of high-fashion eyewear and designer frames, plus prescription sunglasses.

Brands we carry include Ray-Ban, Oakley, Prada, Michael Kors, Calvin Klein, DKNY, Armani Exchange, Silhouette, Face à Face, L.A.M.B. and GX.

06 Eye disease screening & emergencies

Every exam includes screening for eye disease, including cataract and glaucoma screening. Some eye diseases develop slowly without pain or vision loss, so early detection matters. We offer treatment of eye diseases too, not just screening.

We also see patients for eye emergencies, such as a red eye or something stuck in your eye (foreign body removal). Call your nearest office right away.

07 LASIK co-management

Thinking about LASIK? We'll review your history and fully assess your eye health and vision to see if you're a good candidate, then refer you to the LASIK surgery center we've worked with on an ongoing basis.

After surgery, we handle your follow-up visits — typically starting the day after surgery and continuing over the next six months — with a full exam recommended after one year.

08 Pediatric eye care

We take pride in caring for our younger patients in an environment that is lighthearted and fun. Healthy, efficient vision makes the visual demands of learning in school easier.

We take a preventive approach to your child's eye health and will work with your child's primary care physician to help keep their eyes healthy.
