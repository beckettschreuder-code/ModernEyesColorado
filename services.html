What we offer

Eye care for everyday life.

From routine exams to contacts and eye-health screening — clear care without the hard sell. Tap a service below to learn more.

Services

01 Comprehensive eye exams

A full exam looks at how clearly you see and how healthy your eyes are overall. We check your prescription, eye alignment, and the health of the front and back of the eye — then take time to explain what we find in plain language.

Whether you’re due for a yearly visit or noticing new blur, headaches, or eye strain, we’re here to help you understand your options and next steps.

02 Glasses & frames

Once we have an accurate prescription, we’ll help you find frames that fit your face, lifestyle, and budget — from everyday styles to something a little more fun.

Our team can walk you through lens options (like progressive, blue-light filtering, or lighter materials) so you leave with glasses you’ll actually want to wear.

03 Contact lenses

New to contacts or ready for a change? We provide fittings and follow-up so your lenses feel comfortable and give you clear vision throughout the day.

We can discuss soft daily or monthly lenses, and help with common questions about insertion, care, and when contacts (or glasses) may be the better fit for you.

04 Eye disease screening

Regular exams include screening for common eye conditions such as glaucoma, cataracts, and macular degeneration — as well as changes related to diabetes and other health factors.

Early detection matters. If we notice something that needs closer attention, we’ll explain what we see and coordinate follow-up or referral when appropriate.

05 LASIK pre- & post-operative care

Thinking about refractive surgery? We can evaluate whether you may be a candidate and discuss what to expect before and after a procedure.

After surgery, we provide follow-up care in coordination with your surgical team — checking healing, vision, and comfort so you have a familiar office to turn to along the way.

06 Pediatric & family care

Kids’ eyes change quickly. We offer friendly, age-appropriate exams for children and teens — watching for nearsightedness, focusing issues, and other concerns that can affect school and sports.

Families are welcome at all three offices. We’re happy to see parents, kids, and grandparents under one roof so eye care fits into real life.

Need something specific? Call your preferred office — our team can help you know what to expect and which appointment type fits.
