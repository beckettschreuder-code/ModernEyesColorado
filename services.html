What we offer

Eye care for everyday life.

From routine exams to contacts and eye-health screening — clear care without the hard sell.

Services

01

Comprehensive eye exams

Full exams that check how well you see and how healthy your eyes are. We take time to explain findings and answer questions.

02

Glasses & contact lenses

Prescriptions, fittings, and help choosing frames or contacts that fit your life — comfort and clarity first.

03

Eye disease screening

Screening for conditions such as glaucoma, macular degeneration, and diabetic eye disease as part of regular care.

04

LASIK pre- & post-op care

Evaluations before refractive surgery and follow-up care afterward, coordinated with your surgical team when needed.
