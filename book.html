Insurance & payments

Our staff works directly with you and your vision insurer to submit claims and make sure you understand your coverage. Insurance plans we carry include:

  • Medicaid
  • Medicare (Railroad Medicare)
  • UMR
  • UnitedHealthcare

Not sure about your plan? Call your office, such as (303) 773-2020, and we’ll check for you. Many vision plans cover basic vision costs but not everything, and often require a co-pay for the office visit.

Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA): these pre-tax savings plans can be used for eye exams, glasses, contacts and often laser vision surgery, and can typically be used alongside any vision insurance plan. Check with your employer’s benefits administrator.

Ways to pay: Apple Pay, cash, check, Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express and VSP gift certificates. We’re happy to talk about financing options before your exam, including CareCredit. We tell patients about exam and treatment costs at the start of the appointment, and you can ask for a breakdown any time.
