Care for every set of eyes

From routine exams to hard-to-fit contacts, here’s how we can help. Tap any card to learn more.

Eye exams

Comprehensive exams for adults and kids, often drop-free with ultra-wide-field retinal imaging.

Contact lenses

Fittings for daily, extended-wear, gas permeable, cosmetic and hard-to-fit lenses.

Designer eyewear

A full optical department with fashion frames, designer brands and help finding your fit.

Sunglasses

UV-protective and polarized prescription sunglasses, plus clip-ons and shades that darken.

Kids’ eye care

A lighthearted, fun visit that makes learning in school easier.

Eye emergencies

Red eye or something stuck in your eye? Call your nearest office right away.

LASIK co‑management

Evaluation, referral and follow-up care before and after laser surgery.

Eye disease screening

Glaucoma, cataract and macular degeneration testing, plus treatment of eye disease.
